Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video or Short to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

1. Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
2. Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
3. Enter an optional **Name** and paste your video link into the **URL** field (supports regular video links, share links, and YouTube Shorts).
4. (Optional) Adjust **Display options** (Caption, Mute) or **Playback options** (Start At, End At).
5. Click **Save** to create the asset, then assign it to a Screen, Playlist, Schedule, or Split Screen.
