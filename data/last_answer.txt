Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Paste your video link into the **URL** field (supports standard video, share, or Shorts links) and enter an optional **Name**.
* (Optional) Configure **Display options** (captions, mute) or **Playback options** (start/end times).
* Click **Save** to add the video to your assets, then assign it to your screen, playlist, or schedule.
