Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

1. Log in to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
2. Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
3. Paste the YouTube video or Short link into the **URL** field.
4. (Optional) Adjust settings such as **Mute**, **Caption**, or specify **Start At** and **End At** playback times.
5. Click **Save** to save the video asset to your library.
