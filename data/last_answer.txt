Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* **Go to Apps:** Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* **Select YouTube:** Search for "YouTube" and select the **YouTube** app.
* **Enter Details:** Give your video an optional **Name** and paste your YouTube video, share, or Shorts **URL**.
* **Configure Options:** Click **Display options** to toggle Captions or Mute, and use **Playback options** if you only want to play a specific part of the video.
* **Save & Assign:** Click **Save**. Your video is now in **Files/Assets** and can be assigned to your screens, playlists, schedules, or split screens.

For more detailed information, please see the full article:
