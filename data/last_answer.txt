Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

1. Log in to your OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and select **Apps**.
2. Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
3. Paste your video link into the **URL** field (the **Name** field will automatically populate with the video title).
4. (Optional) Configure **Display options** (Caption or Mute) or **Playback options** (Start At / End At times).
5. Click **Save**. Your video asset can now be added to your Screens, Playlists, or Schedules.
