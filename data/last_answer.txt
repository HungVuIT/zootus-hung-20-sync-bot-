Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to your screens, follow these steps:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
* Enter a **Name** (optional) and paste your YouTube video's **URL** (supports regular video, share, or Shorts links).
* Optionally adjust **Display options** (to mute or enable captions) and **Playback options** (to set specific start/end times).
* Click **Save** to add the video to your Files/Assets, where you can then assign it to your screens, playlists, or schedules.
