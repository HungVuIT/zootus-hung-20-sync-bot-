Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
* Enter an optional **Name** and paste your YouTube video link into the **URL** field (supports standard video, share, or Shorts links).
* (Optional) Adjust settings under **Display options** (Caption, Mute) or **Playback options** (Start At, End At).
* Click **Save** to add the video to **Files/Assets**, then assign it to a Screen, Playlist, Schedule, or Split Screen.
