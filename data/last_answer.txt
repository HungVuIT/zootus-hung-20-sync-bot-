Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video in OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Enter an optional **Name** and paste the YouTube video, share, or Shorts **URL**.
* Configure any optional **Display options** (such as captions or mute) and **Playback options** (start/end times).
* Click **Save** to save the YouTube video to your Files/Assets.
