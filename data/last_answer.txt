Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* **Go to Apps:** Log in to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* **Select YouTube:** Search for and click on the **YouTube** app.
* **Enter video info:** Provide an optional **Name** and paste your YouTube **URL** (regular, share, or YouTube Shorts links are supported).
* **Configure options (Optional):** Set display settings like **Caption** or **Mute**, and customize start and end times under **Playback options**.
* **Save & Assign:** Click **Save** to add the video to your Assets, where you can then assign it to your screens, Playlists, or Schedules.
