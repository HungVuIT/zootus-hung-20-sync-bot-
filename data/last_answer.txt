Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to your screens, follow these steps:

* **Go to Apps:** Log in to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click on **Apps**.
* **Select YouTube:** Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
* **Enter Details:** Give your asset a **Name** (optional) and paste the YouTube video **URL** (supports regular, share, or Shorts links; live or embed links are not supported).
* **Adjust Settings:** Customize your setup under **Display options** (to Mute or turn on Captions) and **Playback options** (to set specific Start and End times).
* **Save and Assign:** Click **Save**; your video is now in your **Files/Assets** and can be assigned to your screens, playlists, or schedules.

For more detailed information, please see the full article:
