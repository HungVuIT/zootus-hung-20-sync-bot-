Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Go to **Files/Assets** in the OptiSigns portal and click **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Paste the link to your video (regular, share, or YouTube Shorts link) into the **URL** field and give it a **Name**.
* (Optional) Adjust **Display Options** (mute, captions) or **Playback Options** (set custom start/end times), then click **Save**.
* Assign the saved asset to a **Screen**, **Playlist**, **Schedule**, or **Split Screen** zone.
