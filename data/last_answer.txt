Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

1. Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
2. Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
3. Paste the video link into the **URL** field (a regular video, share, or Shorts link).
4. (Optional) Adjust **Display options** (such as Mute or Caption) or **Playback Options** (to set a custom **Start At** or **End At** time).
5. Click **Save** to create your YouTube asset.
