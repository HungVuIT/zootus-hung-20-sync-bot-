Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Go to **Files/Assets** in the OptiSigns portal and click **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Paste your video link (regular link, share link, or YouTube Shorts link) into the **URL** field.
* (Optional) Adjust **Display Options** (Mute, Captions) or **Playback Options** (Start At / End At times).
* Click **Save**.
