Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video or Short to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Enter an optional **Name** and paste your YouTube video link in the **URL** field (regular, share, or Shorts links are accepted).
* (Optional) Adjust **Display options** (Caption, Mute) or **Playback options** (Start At / End At).
* Click **Save** to add the asset to your **Files/Assets**, then assign it to a screen, playlist, schedule, or split screen.
