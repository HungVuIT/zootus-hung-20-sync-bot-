Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Go to **Files/Assets** in the OptiSigns portal and click **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Paste your public or unlisted video link (regular, share, or Shorts link) into the **URL** field.
* Adjust optional settings like **Name**, **Caption**, **Mute**, or set **Start At** / **End At** playback times, then click **Save**.
* Push the saved asset to your screen via the **Screens** tab, or add it to a playlist or schedule.
