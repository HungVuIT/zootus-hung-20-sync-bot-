Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video in OptiSigns:

1. Log in to your OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and select **Apps**.
2. Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
3. Paste your video link into the **URL** field (regular, share, or Shorts links are supported) and set an asset **Name**.
4. (Optional) Configure settings under **Display options** (Caption, Mute) or **Playback options** (Start At, End At).
5. Click **Save** to create the asset and store it in your Files/Assets.
