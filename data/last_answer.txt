Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video in OptiSigns:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and click the **YouTube** app.
* Enter a **Name** (optional) and paste your YouTube video link into the **URL** field.
* (Optional) Configure settings under **Display options** (Caption, Mute) or **Playback options** (Start At, End At).
* Click **Save**, then assign the asset to your screen, Playlist, or Schedule.
