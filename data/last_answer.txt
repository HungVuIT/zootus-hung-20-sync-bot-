Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
* Enter a **Name** for your asset and paste your video link into the **URL** field (supports standard, share, or YouTube Shorts links).
* (Optional) Adjust **Display options** (captions/mute) or **Playback options** (Start At / End At times).
* Click **Save** to save the video to your assets so you can assign it to a screen, playlist, or schedule.
