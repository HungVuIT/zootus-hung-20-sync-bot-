Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Go to **Files/Assets** in the OptiSigns portal and select **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Paste your video link into the **URL** field (accepts regular video, share, or YouTube Shorts links).
* Adjust optional settings like **Name**, **Display options** (Caption or Mute), or **Playback Options** (Start At and End At times).
* Click **Save** to create your YouTube app asset.
