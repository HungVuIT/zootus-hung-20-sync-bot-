Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns:

* Go to **Files/Assets** in the OptiSigns portal and click **Apps**.
* Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
* Enter an optional **Name** and paste your YouTube link into the **URL** field (supports standard, share, or Shorts links).
* (Optional) Adjust **Display options** (Caption, Mute) or **Playback options** (Start At, End At).
* Click **Save** to store the asset, then assign it to your screens, playlists, or schedules.
