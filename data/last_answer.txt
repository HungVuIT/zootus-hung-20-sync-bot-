Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* **Go to Apps:** Log in to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* **Select YouTube:** Search for "YouTube" and select the **YouTube** app.
* **Enter Details:** Paste your YouTube video, share, or Shorts URL in the URL field, and optionally enter a Name for the asset. 
* **Configure Options (Optional):** You can adjust **Display options** (to toggle captions or mute the audio) and **Playback options** (to specify a custom "Start At" and "End At" time).
* **Save and Assign:** Click **Save**. Your video will now appear in your Files/Assets, ready to be assigned to your screens, playlists, or schedules.

For more details, you can refer to the full guide:
