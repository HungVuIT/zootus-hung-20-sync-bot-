Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

1. Log in to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
2. Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
3. Enter a **Name** (optional) and paste the YouTube video **URL** (supports regular video, share, or Shorts links).
4. (Optional) Configure **Display options** (Captions, Mute) or **Playback options** (Start At, End At).
5. Click **Save** to create the asset, which you can then assign to your screens, playlists, or schedules.
