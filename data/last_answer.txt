Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video in OptiSigns:

1. Log in to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
2. Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
3. Paste your YouTube video link into the **URL** field.
4. (Optional) Set an asset **Name**, toggle **Display options** (Mute/Caption), or adjust **Playback Options** (Start At/End At).
5. Click **Save** to create the asset in your Files/Assets library.
