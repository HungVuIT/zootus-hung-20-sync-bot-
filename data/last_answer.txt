Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns:

1. Log in to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
2. Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
3. Paste your YouTube video or Shorts link into the **URL** field (the **Name** field will automatically populate with the video title).
4. Adjust optional settings such as **Display options** (Caption, Mute) or **Playback Options** (Start At, End At) if needed.
5. Click **Save**, then assign the created asset to your screen, playlist, or schedule.
