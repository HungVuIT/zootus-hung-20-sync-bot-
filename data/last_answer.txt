Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

1. Go to **Files/Assets** in the OptiSigns portal and click **Apps**.
2. Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
3. Enter a **Name** for your asset and paste your video link into the **URL** field.
4. (Optional) Adjust **Display Options** (Caption, Mute) or **Playback Options** (Start At, End At).
5. Click **Save**.
