Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* Search for and select **YouTube**.
* Paste your YouTube video URL (regular video link, share link, or YouTube Shorts link) and enter an optional name.
* (Optional) Configure **Display options** (Caption, Mute) or **Playback options** (Start At, End At).
* Click **Save** to add the video to your asset list, where you can assign it to screens, playlists, or schedules.
