Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Go to **Files/Assets** in the OptiSigns portal and click **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Paste the link to your video or YouTube Short into the **URL** field (the **Name** will auto-fill with the video's title).
* (Optional) Configure **Display options** (Caption, Mute) or **Playback options** (Start At, End At).
* Click **Save** to add the video to your assets.
