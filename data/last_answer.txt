Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Enter an optional **Name** and paste your video or YouTube Shorts link into the **URL** field.
* (Optional) Adjust **Display options** (such as captions or mute) and **Playback options** (custom start and end times).
* Click **Save**, then assign the YouTube asset to your screen, playlist, or schedule.
