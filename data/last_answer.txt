Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to your screens, follow these steps:

* **Access Apps:** Log in to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* **Select YouTube App:** Search for "YouTube" and click on the **YouTube** app.
* **Enter Video Details:** Paste your YouTube link in the **URL** field (supports regular, share, or Shorts links). You can also customize the **Name**.
* **Configure Options (Optional):** Use **Display options** to toggle captions and mute, or **Playback options** to set a specific start and end time.
* **Save and Assign:** Click **Save**. Your video will now be in your Files/Assets, ready to be assigned to screens, playlists, or schedules.
