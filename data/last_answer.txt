Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
* Enter a name for the asset (optional) and paste your YouTube video URL (regular, share, or Shorts links are supported).
* Configure optional settings under **Display options** (such as captions and mute) and **Playback options** (to set specific start and end times).
* Click **Save**, then go to **Screens** to assign the newly created asset to your screen, playlist, or schedule.
