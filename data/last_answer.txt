Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

1. Go to **Files/Assets** in the OptiSigns portal and click **Apps**.
2. Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
3. Paste your public or unlisted YouTube video link (regular video, share link, or Shorts) into the **URL** field.
4. (Optional) Set display options (such as Mute or Captions) or configure Playback Options (**Start At** / **End At**).
5. Click **Save** to create the asset, which you can then assign to a screen, playlist, or schedule.
