Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Go to **Files/Assets** in the OptiSigns portal and click **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Paste your video link into the **URL** field (the **Name** field will fill in automatically with the video title, but you can change it).
* (Optional) Configure **Display Options** (Caption, Mute) and **Playback Options** (Start At, End At times).
* Click **Save** to add the YouTube video to your assets.
