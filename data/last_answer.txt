Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video in OptiSigns:

* Go to **Files/Assets** in the OptiSigns portal and click **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Paste your public or unlisted video link into the **URL** field.
* (Optional) Customize settings under **Display Options** (Caption, Mute) or **Playback Options** (Start At, End At).
* Click **Save** to add the video asset to your account.
