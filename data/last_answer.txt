Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* **Access Apps:** Log in to your OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click on **Apps**.
* **Select the YouTube App:** Search for "YouTube" and select the **YouTube** app.
* **Enter the URL:** Paste your YouTube link (regular video, share, or Shorts link) into the **URL** field, and optionally enter a **Name**.
* **Configure Options (Optional):** Use **Display options** to toggle closed captions or mute the sound, and use **Playback options** to set a custom "Start At" or "End At" time.
* **Save & Assign:** Click **Save** to store the video in your Files/Assets, then assign it directly to your screens, playlists, or schedules.
