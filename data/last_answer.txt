Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Go to **Files/Assets** in the OptiSigns portal and select **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Paste your public or unlisted YouTube video link into the **URL** field.
* (Optional) Adjust **Display options** (Mute or Captions) and **Playback Options** (Start At/End At).
* Click **Save** to create your YouTube asset.
