Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* **Navigate to Apps:** Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* **Select YouTube:** Search for "YouTube" and click on the **YouTube** app.
* **Enter Video Details:** Paste your YouTube link (supports regular, share, or Shorts links) into the **URL** field and enter an optional name. 
* **Configure Options (Optional):** You can adjust *Display options* to toggle Captions or Mute, and use *Playback options* to set custom Start At and End At times.
* **Save and Assign:** Click **Save**. Your video will appear in Files/Assets and is ready to be assigned to your screens, playlists, or schedules.
