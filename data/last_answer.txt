Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Enter a **Name** (optional) and paste your YouTube video, share, or Shorts **URL**.
* (Optional) Adjust **Display options** (Caption, Mute) or **Playback options** (Start At, End At).
* Click **Save** to store the video in your **Files/Assets**, where you can assign it to screens, playlists, or schedules.
