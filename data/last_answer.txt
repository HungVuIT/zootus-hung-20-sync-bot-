Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video or Short to your screens, follow these steps:

* **Step 1:** Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click on **Apps**.
* **Step 2:** Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
* **Step 3:** Enter an optional **Name** and paste your video's link into the **URL** field (regular, share, and Shorts links are supported).
* **Step 4:** Customize your playback under **Display options** (to mute or enable captions) and **Playback options** (to set specific start and end times).
* **Step 5:** Click **Save**; the video will appear in your Files/Assets, where you can assign it directly to your screens, Playlists, or Schedules.
