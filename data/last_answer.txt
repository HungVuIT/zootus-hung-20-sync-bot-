Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Log in to the OptiSigns portal, navigate to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
* Enter an optional **Name** and paste your video link into the **URL** field (supports standard, share, and Shorts links).
* (Optional) Adjust **Display options** (Caption, Mute) and **Playback options** (Start At, End At).
* Click **Save** to store the video in your **Files/Assets** list, where you can assign it to screens, playlists, or schedules.
