Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* Go to **Files/Assets** in the OptiSigns portal and click **Apps**.
* Search for and select the **YouTube** app.
* Paste your video link into the **URL** field (the **Name** will automatically populate with the video's title).
* (Optional) Adjust **Display Options** (Mute/Caption) or **Playback Options** (Start At/End At times).
* Click **Save** to create your asset, which can then be assigned to screens, playlists, or schedules.
