Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns, follow these steps:

* **Open the YouTube App**: Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, click on **Apps**, and select the **YouTube** app.
* **Enter Details**: Give your asset an optional **Name** and paste your video's **URL** (this accepts regular, share, or YouTube Shorts links).
* **Adjust Options**: Under *Display options*, you can toggle **Captions** or **Mute**. Under *Playback options*, you can set specific **Start At** and **End At** times to play only a portion of the video.
* **Save & Assign**: Click **Save** to add the video to your Files/Assets. You can then assign it directly to your screens, or add it to a Playlist, Schedule, or Split Screen.
