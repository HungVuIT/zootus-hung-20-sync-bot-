Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns:

* Go to **Files/Assets** in the OptiSigns portal and click **Apps**.
* Search for **YouTube** and select the **YouTube** app.
* Paste the link to your public or unlisted YouTube video in the **URL** field.
* Adjust optional display settings (such as **Mute** or **Caption**) and playback settings (such as **Start At** or **End At** times).
* Click **Save**.
