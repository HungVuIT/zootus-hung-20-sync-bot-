Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to your OptiSigns screen, follow these steps:

* **Access Apps:** Log in to your OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* **Select YouTube:** Search for "YouTube" and click on the **YouTube** app.
* **Enter Details:** Input a **Name** (optional) and paste your video link in the **URL** field (regular video links, share links, and YouTube Shorts are supported).
* **Configure Options:** Customize your settings under **Display options** (such as toggling Captions or Muting the video) or **Playback options** (to set specific start and end times).
* **Save & Assign:** Click **Save**. You can now assign the video directly to your screens, or add it to a Playlist or Schedule.
