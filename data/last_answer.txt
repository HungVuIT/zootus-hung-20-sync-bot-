Q: How do I add a YouTube video?

To add a YouTube video to OptiSigns:

* **Go to Apps:** Log in to the OptiSigns portal, go to **Files/Assets**, and click **Apps**.
* **Select YouTube:** Search for "YouTube" and select the **YouTube** app.
* **Enter Video Details:** Paste your YouTube video link in the **URL** field and give it an optional **Name**.
* **Configure Options (Optional):** Set your preferred display options (Caption, Mute) or adjust playback times (Start At, End At).
* **Save & Assign:** Click **Save** to add the video to your Files/Assets, then assign it to a screen, playlist, schedule, or split screen.
